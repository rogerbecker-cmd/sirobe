SIROBE Privacy Policy

As of 24 November 2026

In short: SIROBE collects no data. What you enter stays on your Mac or in folders you choose yourself.

Controller

Roger Becker, Oberfeldstrasse 30, 8586 Riedt b. Erlen, Switzerland – roger.becker@bluewin.ch

Data processed by SIROBE

The company, customer, item and document data you enter. They are kept in a database on your Mac or in folders you choose yourself (project files, backups, exports, shared data set).

The provider never receives these data. SIROBE has no server, no user account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Cloud services

If you put files in a cloud folder (e.g. Dropbox, OneDrive, Google Drive), the terms of that service apply. SIROBE itself sends nothing over the internet.

For the shared data set, SIROBE writes the name of this Mac into the lock file in the chosen folder, so other Macs can see where the data set is open.

E-mail and printing

When sending by e-mail, SIROBE creates a draft in your mail program; it is sent only when you send it there.

Purchase and subscription

Purchases and subscriptions are handled by Apple (App Store). SIROBE only learns whether a purchase or subscription exists – no payment or personal data. Apple's privacy policy applies.

Your rights

As the provider processes none of your personal data, there is nothing to hand over or delete on their side. You delete your data in SIROBE or by deleting the files.

Contact
