Frequently asked questions

What do I need?

A Mac with macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later. No account is needed; internet only for the purchase and so the App Store can confirm the subscription.

Where is my data?

On your Mac, in the app's data folder (Settings › Backup shows it with the magnifier). Project files, backups and the shared data set go wherever you choose.

What happens without a subscription?

SIROBE switches to read-only mode: viewing, printing, exporting and backing up still work, adding and changing do not. Your data stays complete.

How do I cancel the subscription?

In the settings of your Apple Account under Subscriptions – or in SIROBE under Unlock SIROBE › Manage Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the period.

I have a new Mac.

Download SIROBE from the App Store and click «Restore Purchases» in the window «Unlock SIROBE» (same Apple Account). Bring your data along with a backup or the shared data set.

I used «Rechnung» 1.x before.

On the first launch SIROBE takes over the data automatically. If project files lie elsewhere, SIROBE asks for the folder once. Make a backup first and delete the old program afterwards.
