SIROBE

Invoicing for Swiss small businesses and fiduciaries – for the Mac.

Quotes, invoices with the Swiss QR payment part, payment matching, dunning and VAT returns. Your data stays on your Mac.

  • Quote, order confirmation, invoice, credit note and cancellation – with the Swiss QR payment part following the SIX standard
  • Read the bank file (camt.053/054): payments are matched to invoices automatically
  • Dunning with fees and default interest, recurring invoices
  • VAT report (effective method or net tax rate) and CSV export for your accounting
  • Documents in the customer's language: German, French, Italian, English
  • No account, no data collection; shared data set for several Macs via Dropbox, OneDrive or Google Drive
In the Mac App Store (Switzerland): yearly or half-yearly subscription with one week free, or a one-time purchase.
